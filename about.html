About SeedCore

Applications for places, journeys and real things.

SeedCore creates application experiences for city discovery, family travel, personal souvenirs, rare collectibles and friendly robots.

Where it comes to life

On city streets, inside museums and resorts, during family activities, across a collector handover, and beside a helpful tabletop robot.

What connects the work

A place to understand. Something to create. A next step to trust.

Places

Make a city feel discoverable.

I’m interested in local makers, workshops, attractions and public stories that are difficult to find through a generic search box.

People

Give families a real role.

Children can make choices and create. Parents keep approval, privacy and payment. The product should feel joyful to both.

Real things

Help the physical story stay clear.

Whether it is a rare shoe, a souvenir or a robot action, the application should help a person understand what they are seeing and what happens next.

The experience

Discover, create and carry something meaningful forward.

Each application helps a person move naturally from curiosity to participation and then to a clear, memorable outcome.

01

Discover.

Find a local place, a destination story, a creative theme or the history of a treasured object.

02

Participate.

Choose a route, design a souvenir, play through an activity or follow a physical journey.

03

Remember.

Leave with a personal object, a family memory, a trusted result or a new connection to the place.
